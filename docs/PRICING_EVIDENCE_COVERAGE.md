# iPhone evidence engine: coverage and error limits

Generated by `scripts/pricing-evidence/coverage-limits.ts` from the pooled held-out rows of protocols 1 and 2 (every row predicted without seeing its own record) under what `scope.json` serves. Quotes are Cashify quotes dated 16-24 Oct 2026; the data is ~30 condition shapes repeated across storages, so effective sample sizes are far below the row counts.

**What these numbers do and do not say.**
- *Coverage* = share of held-out requests the shipped scope answers with a price from the evidence model, from the existing engine, or by sending the customer to inspection.
- *Estimate error* = engine Cashify-equivalent vs the observed Cashify base price, as % of max(observed base, 20% of Get Upto), only for rows the evidence model priced.
- *Observed-ceiling breach* = the customer payout (after the uplift, the INR 99 fee and the ceiling function, no coupon) is above Cashify's ACTUAL payout + min(8%, INR 2,000). The ceiling function guarantees the limit against the ENGINE'S estimate only; this column is what the business rule is really about, and it is only as good as the estimate. No hard Cashify ceiling is claimed.
